## Abstract (v0.1)

Smallholder yields in Mozambique remain far below agronomic potential. Many interventions and large commercial attempts fail under logistics, cost, land conflict, thin markets, weak training, and theft. GroundYield proposes a **different bet**: **small modular farm units** (~0.25–0.5 ha), **public costs and failures**, **local baselines before impact claims**, and optional AI/connectivity only as support.  

As of this version there are **zero** GroundYield units and **zero** pilot baselines. The operator discloses a prior large-scale farming failure in the same geography. This paper will be rewritten as field data arrives — including if the thesis fails.

## 1. Problem

### 1.1 Yield gap

Published figures place smallholder maize in Mozambique commonly in the order of **~0.4–1.3 t/ha** (often near **~1 t/ha** nationally), well below what improved water, seed, and fertility can deliver in better-supported systems. See [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

**Important:** national averages **justify why a pilot exists**. They do **not** substitute for **local baselines** when claiming impact ([CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md)).

### 1.2 Why interventions fail (pattern, not one villain)

History and secondary literature point to recurring modes ([WHY_OTHERS_FAILED.md](WHY_OTHERS_FAILED.md)):

- Infrastructure and logistics that never arrive on schedule  
- Credit and cost ramps mid-build  
- Land and community conflict; outsider-only control  
- Skills mismatch and “start from zero” underestimated  
- Technology drops (e.g. solar pumps) with **little training** and high **theft**  
- Thin offtake markets  
- Mega-programmes that under-deliver  

The operator’s own prior large commercial path in the Vilanculos area **failed and was left**, with an operator-disclosed loss order of **~ZAR 10 million** ([LESSONS_MZ.md](LESSONS_MZ.md)). GroundYield is explicitly **not** a silent empire rebuild.

### 1.3 Trust gap

Development and ag projects often lose public trust via ghost units, fake baselines, cherry-picked yields, and silent number changes ([INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md)). Any new pilot that ignores that pattern deserves skepticism.

## 2. Thesis

**Claim (to be tested, not assumed):**

> A **standardized modular package** (solar irrigation + improved inputs + storage + optional livestock), run on **community-legitimate small plots**, with **public measurement and stop rules**, can raise household productivity and generate **replicable cost/yield data** — even if absolute “success” is modest — better than opaque large-scale or unmeasured gift projects.

**Secondary claims (also testable):**

1. **Few units done honestly** beat many units on paper.  
2. **Theft and training** are first-class design problems, not footnotes ([SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md)).  
3. **AI and Starlink** help choice, data, and school access — they do **not** replace water and seed ([SYSTEM.md](SYSTEM.md), [AI_FIELD_PATH.md](AI_FIELD_PATH.md)).  
4. **Open failure** is an asset for the next community ([VALUE_AND_MONEY.md](VALUE_AND_MONEY.md)).

## 3. Method

### 3.1 Physical package (core)

Per modular unit (~0.25–0.5 ha) — detail [UNIT.md](UNIT.md):

| Element | Role |
|---------|------|
| Solar irrigation | Water when rain fails |
| Improved seed + micro-dose fertilizer | Yield response without blanket excess |
| Hermetic storage | Cut post-harvest loss |
| Optional poultry/goats | Income diversification where markets allow |
| Phone agronomy | After review gates only |

Planning cost band (Year 0, first unit): order **~$1,270–$2,985 USD** (planning only until field quotes).

## 5. What success looks like (and what does not)

| Success | Not success |
|---------|-------------|
| Public baselines and season data (good or bad) | Pretty website only |
| ≥1 real registry unit with trained custodian | “30 units” with no rows |
| Honest cost actuals | Planning band sold as proven |
| Community can refuse or exit with dignity | Debt traps / join-up fees |
| Others can learn from our autopsy | Hidden failure |

Aspirational design aims (30–60 units, large % uplift) remain **hypotheses** until measured ([CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md)).

## 7. Open questions (research agenda)

1. What are **local** staple yields (method + F/E) around Rumbacaca?  
2. What water sources are real at candidate plots?  
3. What is the **local theft** pattern for panels/pumps?  
4. Who buys surplus, at what farm-gate price (date/place F)?  
5. Which **phone apps** do people already use for rain/planting?  
6. Does a modular unit clear costs in Season 1 under real prices?  
7. Can AI CHOICE demos improve decisions without harm?  
8. When (if ever) do sensors beat a weekly phone photo for verification?

Answers will rewrite §4–5 and the [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
