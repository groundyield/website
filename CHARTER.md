# GroundYield Public Charter

**What is binding vs aspirational (read this first)**

| Kind | What it covers |
|------|----------------|
| **Binding process** | How we publish, measure, correct, and refuse fraud — always |
| **Aspirational design targets** | Season 1 scale and yield **goals** — not proven results |

We do **not** treat design targets as achieved outcomes. No impact claim is valid without a dated **local baseline** and public method ([INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md), [DATA.md](DATA.md)).

---

## Binding process commitments

1. **Transparency**  
   All material costs, yields, incomes, and failures related to pilot units will be published. No private performance reports.

2. **Modularity**  
   Units are standardized and designed so others can replicate them without proprietary lock-in.

3. **Local first**  
   Land and farming are done with local communities and smallholders, not imported labor models.

4. **Measurement before claims**  
   Baseline data is collected before claiming impact. Results are compared against local reality, not theoretical potential alone.

5. **Open by default**  
   Design documents, methods, and lessons are public unless there is a clear privacy or safety reason not to publish.

6. **Integrity over optics**  
   We design against ghost units, fake baselines, cherry-picked yields, hidden related-party deals, and silent rewrites. Full policy: [INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md). Anyone may challenge a published number; material corrections are dated and public.

7. **No household left on a fake promise**  
   We do not enroll households into dependency on kit or advice we cannot support. If self-funding stops, we say so publicly and do not invent continuity ([WHO.md](WHO.md)).

## Season 1 design targets (aspirational — not yet measured)

These are **planning goals** for design and fundraising honesty, **not** field results:

- **Aim:** on the order of **30–60** modular units in the Vilanculos area *if* land, partners, water, and capital allow  
- **Aim:** large yield gains on staples vs **local baseline** (order of **80–100%+** is a *hypothesis to test*, not a guarantee)  
- **Aim:** measurable household income change via cash crops and/or livestock modules *where markets work*  
- **Commit:** a public dataset of costs, yields, and outcomes for every unit that actually runs  

[ECONOMICS.md](ECONOMICS.md) states that staple uplift alone may not carry the model — dry-season vegetables / tourism demand must be checked on the ground. Until those prices and volumes are **F**-grade local facts, income claims stay provisional.

### How this coexists with “commercial farming is near-impossible”

The operator’s lived view ([LESSONS_MZ.md](LESSONS_MZ.md)): **large-scale commercial** farming under the conditions he hit felt **structurally very hard**.

**What smaller scale does *not* automatically fix:** the same mechanisms still kill a modular unit. [RISK_AND_GAPS.md](RISK_AND_GAPS.md) scores **theft (T1/T2)** and **water (A1)** at priority **12** — top tier. A stolen ~$1–2k kit is still **100% loss of that unit’s capital**. Modular **bounds dollars at risk per bet** and may speed learning; it is **not** a demonstrated proof that “near impossible” becomes easy. That claim would need **field seasons**, not a reframe.

| Lived | Honest modular stance |
|-------|------------------------|
| Empire commercial near-impossible | **Hypothesis:** tiny measured packages may still help food/learning |
| Not a guarantee of modular success | Aims 30–60 / large % uplift stay **aspirational** |
| If markets/capital fail | Success can be few units + open autopsy (Plan B) — not fake scale |

See [STRATEGIC_GAPS.md](STRATEGIC_GAPS.md) §4.

## What This Charter Does Not Claim

- It does not claim the model is already proven at scale  
- It does not claim zero failures  
- It does not claim any farmer has been enrolled or any unit installed (as of last update: **none**)  
- It does not claim to solve world hunger in one pilot  
- It does not claim legal review in Mozambique is complete  

It claims only this: we will run a transparent, measurable, replicable pilot **when field conditions allow**, and publish results — including the ugly ones — under [INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md).
