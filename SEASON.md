**GroundYield planning reference**  
Vilanculos / Inhambane Province, Mozambique  
Last updated: 27 July 2026

Planning ranges only — not local baselines. Real calendars, varieties, and yields will be rewritten from field interviews and published in `data/`.

---

## Climate context (southern Mozambique)

| Factor | Planning note |
|--------|----------------|
| Rainy season | Roughly **Nov – Mar** (main rains); local variation is large |
| Dry season | Roughly **Apr – Oct** — irrigation matters most here |
| Risk | Late rains, dry spells, cyclone seasons on the coast |
| Implication | Solar irrigation turns dry months into production months for vegetables and second maize cycles |

GroundYield’s modular unit is built so **rainfed staples improve** *and* **irrigated cash crops** fill dry-season market gaps (including tourism demand in Vilanculos).

---

## Priority crops for Season 1 design

| Crop | Role | Why it matters |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Maize** | Staple | Food security; clearest yield comparison vs local baseline |
| **Cassava** | Staple / buffer | Drought resilience; widespread smallholder crop |
| **Leafy veg / tomato / onion** (mix) | Cash | Tourism + local markets; high response to irrigation |
| **Cowpea / beans** | Rotation / protein | Soil and diet diversity |
| **Poultry** (optional module) | Cash / protein | Fast cycles; does not replace crop core |

Exact mix per unit will be farmer-chosen within a **measured package** (same cost and data rules).

## Indicative calendar (Vilanculos area)

Months are **indicative**. Confirm locally before planting claims.

| Month | Rainfed maize / staples | Irrigated veg / cash | Operations focus |
|-------|-------------------------|----------------------|------------------|
| **Sep** | Land prep begins | Nursery / early plantings | Borehole/pump checks; seed orders |
| **Oct** | Final prep | Plant high-value veg | Fertilizer + micro-dose plan |
| **Nov** | Plant maize (if rains start) | Irrigate as needed | Baseline photos + sowing dates logged |
| **Dec** | Weeding / top-dress | Harvest early greens | Pest scouting |
| **Jan** | Crop care | Continuous harvest cycles | Mid-season yield checks |
| **Feb** | Crop care / early harvest zones | Market sales | Track prices (farm-gate) |
| **Mar** | Main maize harvest window | Rotate beds | Post-harvest: dry + hermetic store |
| **Apr** | Store grain; residue management | Dry-season plantings expand | Loss measurement starts |
| **May–Jul** | Off-season planning | Peak irrigation production | Poultry cycles if module active |
| **Aug** | Next-season prep | Seedling production | Publish season numbers |

## What “success” looks like per crop class

| Class | Metric (publish) | Not yet claimed |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| Staples | kg/ha or local unit vs **same-plot or neighbor baseline** | National “potential yield” only |
| Cash veg | kg sold + revenue + input cost | Tourism demand without price evidence |
| Storage | % loss estimate before/after hermetic | Zero-loss marketing |
| Poultry | birds, mortality, feed cost, sales | Guaranteed margins |

Charter rule: **baseline first**, then intervention season.

## Labor & household rhythm (design assumption)

- Peak labor: planting and weeding windows  
- Irrigation reduces some drought risk but **adds pump/maintenance tasks**  
- Advice channel must fit **WhatsApp-first, low-bandwidth** phone use  
- Women and youth roles vary by household — record who does what when collecting baselines (with consent)
