Problem & approach

Smallholder maize in Mozambique is often ~0.4–1.3 t/ha (near ~1 t/ha nationally) — below what water, seed, and fertility can deliver. Sources: SOURCES.md. Most projects are opaque. Large commercial bets also fail (logistics, cost, land, theft).

Our bet: small modular units, local baselines before any % claim, public failures. Optional AI and Starlink support the farm — they are not the product.

Core package

  • Solar-powered irrigation
  • Improved seed + micro-dose fertilizer
  • Phone agronomy (demos open; live rates after review)
  • Hermetic storage · optional poultry/goats

BOM & costs: UNIT.md · Calendar: SEASON.md · Economics (illustrative): ECONOMICS.md

Charter (short)

  • Binding: publish costs, yields, failures; baseline before impact %
  • Aim (not measured): ~30–60 units if land/water/capital allow
  • Aim (hypothesis): large yield gains vs local baseline
  • Open model others can copy or reject with eyes open
