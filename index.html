The Problem

Smallholder maize yields in Mozambique are commonly reported around 0.4–1.3 t/ha (often near ~1 t/ha nationally) — well below regional peers and far below what improved seed, water, and basic fertility can deliver. Sources: FAO factsheet; SOURCES.md. The gap is not primarily knowledge or effort; it is reliable water, improved inputs, basic storage, and timely advice. Most interventions remain opaque and non-replicable. Our impact claims will use local baselines, not national averages alone.

The Approach

Deploy standardized modular farm units that close the yield gap with measurable results. Every cost, yield, income figure, and failure is published. The model is designed to be copied.

Public Charter

  • Process (binding): publish costs, yields, failures; integrity over optics; baseline before impact claims
  • Design aim (not measured): on the order of 30–60 units in Vilanculos if land, water, partners, and capital allow
  • Design aim (hypothesis): large staple yield gains vs local baseline — test, don’t assume
  • Design aim: income via cash crops / poultry where markets actually work
  • Open, replicable model anyone can copy

Full anti-fraud policy: INTEGRITY.md · Challenge a number: open an issue or team@groundyield.org

Fighting fraud from day one

Development projects lose trust in predictable ways: units that exist only on paper, baselines invented after harvest, pretty photos from somewhere else, and numbers that quietly change. GroundYield treats that as a design problem, not a PR problem.

  • Named human accountable for every published figure (Jacques Theron)
  • Baseline before impact — no % win without a dated local baseline
  • Truth marks F / E / H (fact · estimate · hearsay) in the field
  • Failures stay public — dead ends are logged, not deleted
  • No silent rewrites — corrections are dated in UPDATES.md
  • Headline claims need dual evidence (weigh / photo set / witness / receipt)
  • Unit registry before “N units deployed” — live count: units.html (0)
  • We never ask households for a join-up fee via random agents — impostors go to team@groundyield.org

Full threat model and controls: INTEGRITY.md. Money rules and conflicts: WHO.md.

Core Package

  • Solar-powered irrigation
  • Improved / hybrid seeds + micro-dosing fertilizer
  • Phone-based AI agronomy advice
  • Hermetic storage to cut post-harvest loss
  • Optional poultry & goat modules

Full bill of materials and cost bands: UNIT.md (~$1,270–$2,985 per first modular unit, planning ranges).
