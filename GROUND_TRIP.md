## In Field (Mozambique focus)

### Community & land
- [ ] Meet local leaders / associations with introductions, not cold drops
- [ ] Clarify tenure and access models (lease, partnership, communal)
- [ ] Document consent process for public data and photos
- [ ] Note who must be involved for a legitimate pilot

### Baseline agriculture
- [ ] Staple crops grown locally (maize, cassava, others)
- [ ] Typical yields (local units + convert to kg/ha where possible)
- [ ] Rainfed vs irrigated share
- [ ] Input use today (seed type, fertilizer, labor)
- [ ] Post-harvest losses and storage methods
- [ ] Market access: farm-gate prices, tourism demand, middlemen

### Water & energy
- [ ] Groundwater / surface water options
- [ ] Solar pump suppliers and installers in region
- [ ] Rough **quoted** cost bands for a modular irrigation kit (replace UNIT.md v0)

### Supply chain
- [ ] Improved / hybrid seed availability and cost
- [ ] Fertilizer (type, price, reliability)
- [ ] Hermetic storage bags / silos availability
- [ ] Poultry / goat input markets (if modules planned early)

### Risks observed
- [ ] Climate / drought history (anecdotal + local knowledge)
- [ ] Theft, conflict, access issues
- [ ] Currency, import, and logistics friction
