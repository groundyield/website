Day one, in public — what GroundYield is and is not

This project is one day old as a public stack: domain, GitHub identity, website, and open documents landed in the same window. That belongs at the top — not in a footnote.

GroundYield is an attempt at a modular smallholder farm pilot near Vilanculos, Inhambane Province, Mozambique, with costs, yields, and failures published openly. Operator: Jacques Theron. Self-funded. No legal entity yet. No local partner named on the accountability page, because none has agreed in public.

One line: nothing has been measured on a pilot plot. Zero units installed. Zero local baselines. Zero written supplier quotes. Trip dates open.

What exists

  • Public site and repo: charter, integrity rules, Portuguese page, offline field pack
  • Public unit registry with count 0: units.html
  • Cost bands labeled as planning — not field quotes
  • Season 1 scale/yield figures: design aims, not results

What does not exist

  • Land access secured
  • Farmers enrolled
  • Live agronomy WhatsApp (scripts frozen until review)
  • Independent auditor or NGO co-signer
  • Disclosed multi-month runway figure (stop scenario is described; dollars still a gap)

Why publish before the trip

Most projects write measurement rules after the first good photo. We are trying the opposite: anti-fraud and data rules first, so inventing wins has something public to collide with.

  • Baseline before impact claims
  • Truth marks: fact / estimate / hearsay
  • No silent rewrites — dated corrections
  • Dual evidence before headline yield claims
  • Unit counts only from the registry

Full policy: INTEGRITY.md

Adversarial review (what it got right)

Day-one critique: docs ahead of ground contact; self-referential trust; targets before feasibility; early “shipped” claims briefly outran live HTTP 200s; agronomy drafts must not go live without review; households must not be enrolled into silent abandonment.

Those points were substantially right. Deploy is healthy now (production equals main). Strategy risk remains: more markdown does not create a pilot.

AI disclosure

Much of the documentation and site work was AI-assisted (including tools such as Grok), under my direction. I am accountable for what is published. If the prose looks machine-helped, it often was. Concealment would be worse. The test is honest stage and numbers — and public corrections when we fail.

What we are asking for

Not money in this post.

  • Serious local introductions near Vilanculos (with consent)
  • An agronomist to review WhatsApp drafts before any farmer is messaged
  • Written quotes for irrigation and inputs
  • Critical readers of UNIT, ECONOMICS, INTEGRITY
