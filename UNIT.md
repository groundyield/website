# Modular Unit Design v0

**GroundYield public specification**  
Vilanculos, Inhambane Province, Mozambique  
Last updated: 27 July 2026

This is version 0. It is intentionally rough. Costs and design will be refined with real supplier quotes and field conditions during the ground trip. All numbers are public estimates for transparency and replication.

---

## 1. Unit Definition

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Name** | Core Modular Unit (CMU) |
| **Target size** | 0.25 – 0.5 hectare |
| **Primary purpose** | Staple + high-value cash crops under solar irrigation |
| **Design goal** | Standardized, replicable, measurable package a smallholder or group can adopt |
| **Livestock** | Optional add-on modules (poultry first, goats later) |

Why this size?
- Low enough capital to start many units
- Large enough to show meaningful yield and income change
- Fits typical smallholder land access patterns in southern Mozambique
- Easy to scale by adding identical units

## 5. Design Principles (locked)

1. **Modular** — every unit uses the same core package so results are comparable.
2. **Measurable** — baseline yield and costs recorded before claiming impact.
3. **Local first** — land, labor, and as much equipment as possible sourced locally.
4. **Transparent** — this BOM and all future cost updates are public.
5. **Failure-aware** — if a component fails or is too expensive, it is documented and changed openly.

---

## 6. What Will Be Validated on the Ground Trip

- Actual water source depth / distance (determines pump type)
- Local solar kit suppliers and real delivered prices in Inhambane
- Seed and fertilizer availability + prices in Vilanculos / nearby markets
- Current smallholder yields (maize, cassava, vegetables)
- Interest and land access of potential first households
- Poultry input availability and market for eggs/meat

Until then, treat all figures above as **planning ranges**, not commitments.

## 7. Replication Note

Anyone can copy this structure.  
When you implement a unit, publish:
- Final BOM and actual costs
- Baseline yield
- Season results
- What broke or changed

That is how the model improves.
