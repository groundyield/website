## Maize yield gap (Mozambique)

**Site claim (paraphrase):** Smallholder maize yields in Mozambique are often on the order of ~0.8–1 t/ha, far below potential under improved water, seed, and fertility.

| Source | What it supports |
|--------|------------------|
| [FAO Mozambique factsheet](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/templates/tc/tce/pdf/Mozambique_factsheet.pdf) | Main food crop yields: **maize 0.4–1.3 t/ha** (among other staples) |
| [MADER / Amaral et al. analysis of maize 2000–2018](https://www.agricultura.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Analysis-of-maize-production-and-yield-in-Mozambique-2000-2018.pdf) | National average cited near **~0.96 t/ha** in that analysis context |
| [UNU-WIDER IGM agricultural sector report (2025)](https://www.wider.unu.edu/sites/default/files/Blog/PDF/IGM-report-agicultural-sector-2025-eng.pdf) | Smallholder maize productivity in Mozambique described as low (~**1 t/ha** order of magnitude vs higher yields in Ethiopia/South Africa); FAOSTAT-based comparisons |

**How we use this:** National/range figures justify *why* a pilot exists. **Season 1 impact claims** will use **local baselines** measured with participating units, not these secondary sources alone.
