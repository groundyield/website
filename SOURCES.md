## Maize yield gap (Mozambique)

**Site claim (paraphrase):** Smallholder maize yields in Mozambique are often on the order of ~0.8–1 t/ha, far below potential under improved water, seed, and fertility.

| Source | What it supports |
|--------|------------------|
| [FAO Mozambique factsheet](https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/templates/tc/tce/pdf/Mozambique_factsheet.pdf) | Main food crop yields: **maize 0.4–1.3 t/ha** (among other staples) |
| [MADER / Amaral et al. analysis of maize 2000–2018](https://www.agricultura.gov.mz/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Analysis-of-maize-production-and-yield-in-Mozambique-2000-2018.pdf) | National average cited near **~0.96 t/ha** in that analysis context |
| [UNU-WIDER IGM agricultural sector report (2025)](https://www.wider.unu.edu/sites/default/files/Blog/PDF/IGM-report-agicultural-sector-2025-eng.pdf) | Smallholder maize productivity in Mozambique described as low (~**1 t/ha** order of magnitude vs higher yields in Ethiopia/South Africa); FAOSTAT-based comparisons |

**How we use this:** National/range figures justify *why* a pilot exists. **Season 1 impact claims** will use **local baselines** measured with participating units, not these secondary sources alone.

## Why many agri projects fail (Mozambique) — entry points

**Site claim (paraphrase):** Commercial settler waves, land mega-deals, and tech-drop projects often fail under logistics, credit, land conflict, thin markets, weak training, and theft — not because small modular open pilots were tried and disproven. See [WHY_OTHERS_FAILED.md](WHY_OTHERS_FAILED.md).

| Source | What it supports |
|--------|------------------|
| [García et al., *Ecology & Society* — investment frontier in Niassa](https://ecologyandsociety.org/vol27/iss1/art40/) | **Mosagrius / SACADA**-type SA commercial wave: infrastructure promises unmet, credit not delivered, language/skills mismatch, land/labour conflict; many left within ~2 years |
| Contemporary reporting on Mosagrius fiasco (e.g. Africa Intelligence / IOL archives — search “Mosagrius”) | Programme-level failure narrative; do not invent headcounts without a primary cite |
| Academic / policy work on **Zimbabwean commercial farmers in Manica** (Hammar; Hanlon/Smart “Manica miracle” debates) | Boom–bust of cross-border commercial farming; capital and institutional fragility |
| Contested mega-programmes (e.g. **ProSavana** literature; failed concession reporting) | Large land investments can stall, face resistance, or under-deliver planted area / employment vs promise |
| Oakland Institute / GRAIN / [farmlandgrab.org](https://www.farmlandgrab.org/) collections on MZ | Entry points for abandoned or contested land deals — verify each claim before quoting acreage |
| Operator lived experience | [LESSONS_MZ.md](LESSONS_MZ.md) — ~ZAR 10m order loss (E); not a claim of current land control |

**How we use this:** Design against **known failure modes**. We do **not** claim “GroundYield cannot fail.”

## Solar irrigation: theft, training, barriers (Mozambique)

**Site claim (paraphrase):** Theft of panels/pumps is a documented constraint for solar irrigation in Mozambique; mass installs without training fail quickly; portable/removable systems reduce exposure. Controls: [SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md).

| Source | What it supports |
|--------|------------------|
| [Energypedia — Solar Powered Irrigation in Mozambique: Barriers](https://energypedia.info/wiki/Solar_Powered_Irrigation_in_Mozambique_:_Barriers) | Surveys/reports: **theft risk is an important constraint**; fixed submersible panel arrays prone to theft; **suction pumps** allow panels to be taken home; repairs hard; **large projects with many pumps and little training “usually lead to failure within a very short time”** |
| [Practica — Solar irrigation market analysis Mozambique (2021 PDF)](https://www.practica.org/wp-content/uploads/Solar-irrigation-market-analysis-Mozambique-2021.pdf) | Underlying market study for the energypedia series (theft, training, finance, supply chain) |
| [Energypedia — SPIS Support Initiatives (MZ)](https://energypedia.info/wiki/Solar_Powered_Irrigation_in_Mozambique_:_SPIS_Support_Initiatives) | Scale of government vs private pump distributions (order-of-magnitude context only — re-check numbers before public use) |
| [GGGI Mozambique off-grid country brief](https://gggi.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Mozambique-Country-Brief.pdf) | Broader note that solar PV systems can be **susceptible to theft** in off-grid contexts |
| Portable pump design practice (e.g. UNIDO/ADPP SF1-type portable kits) | Regionally, **portability** is used partly to manage theft and seasonal use — validate local stock on the ground trip |

**How we use this:** Prefer **stake before steel**, removable kit where risk is high, few units first, named custodians, public serials — **not** armed-guard branding as the default.
