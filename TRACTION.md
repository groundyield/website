# Traction — How GroundYield gets attention without hype

**Public strategy document**  
Last updated: 27 July 2026

We want people who can help or copy the model to find us.  
We do **not** want viral attention that forces us to perform results that do not yet exist.

This page is the rulebook. It is public so anyone can call us out if we break it.

---

## Core rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Never claim a result that is not measured and published.**  
   No “we will raise yields 100%”. Only “we are targeting X vs local baseline and will publish the numbers either way.”

2. **Lead with process and failure, not vision.**  
   The differentiator is radical transparency + open design, not a new miracle package.

3. **No manufactured urgency or scarcity.**  
   No “limited spots”, “last chance”, countdown timers, or FOMO language.

4. **Name the operator and the stage honestly.**  
   Always: Jacques Theron, self-funded, no legal entity yet, foundation phase, ground trip still ahead.

5. **Prefer durable attention over spikes.**  
   One thoughtful thread that gets saved and shared by the right 50 people is worth more than 10k low-quality impressions.

6. **Document everything we do here.**  
   If we change promotion tactics, this file is updated.

| Audience | Why they matter | What they need to see |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Practical agronomists & extension people in southern Africa | They can stress-test the unit design and baselines | UNIT.md, IRRIGATION.md, local baseline discipline |
| Open-source / open-hardware people | They understand “publish the BOM and the failures” | GitHub, UNIT BOM CSVs, INTEGRITY.md |

| Serious smallholders and farmer associations | Future operators of the model | Real cost bands, no magic, clear partnership draft |

Secondary (useful later, not primary now): general tech Twitter, climate Twitter, impact investors. We do not optimize for them yet.

## X (@GroundYield) strategy

X is currently the highest-leverage channel because the project is still mostly documentation and the founder is active there.

### Posting cadence
- **Default:** 3–5 high-signal posts per week during foundation phase.
- Quality over volume. One strong thread > five mediocre posts.
- When on the ground trip: daily or near-daily short field notes (what was seen, what was quoted, what failed).

### Content pillars (rotate these)

1. **Process & transparency**  
   “Here is the exact BOM. Here is the cost band. Here is how we will measure. Here is how we will handle fraud.”  
   Link to UNIT.md / INTEGRITY.md / DATA.md.

2. **Honest stage updates**  
   “Still foundation phase. No units in the ground. Trip planned. Dates still open.”  
   Never invent progress.

3. **Specific technical details**  
   Irrigation trade-offs, hermetic storage realities, micro-dosing practicalities, WhatsApp advice design.  
   People who know farming respect concrete details.

4. **Anti-hype / anti-fraud stance**  
   Explicitly call out common failure modes in the sector (ghost units, photo theater, national averages instead of local baselines).  
   This attracts the right people and repels the wrong ones.

5. **Field notes (once trip starts)**  
   Short, factual, dated. Quotes, prices, baseline observations, problems.  
   Publish the same day or next day when possible.

### Format preferences
- Threads for anything longer than 280 characters that has a point.
- Always include the relevant GitHub or groundyield.org link.
- Prefer plain language + one concrete number or document over slogans.
- Quote-tweet or reply to relevant technical people when we have something real to add (not engagement farming).

### What we will not do on X
- Paid amplification / ads until we have real Season 1 data.
- Giveaways, contests, or “retweet to win”.
- Fake urgency or “we’re almost there” language before we are.
- Tagging large accounts repeatedly hoping for a boost.
- Posting the same promotional text across many accounts.

### Reply & engagement rules
- Reply substantively to good-faith questions, especially technical or Mozambican ones.
- Ignore pure engagement bait and low-effort criticism that does not engage with the documents.
- When someone finds an error, thank them publicly and correct in UPDATES.md.

## Long-form / blogs / Substack-style writing

We do not need a separate blog platform yet. The GitHub docs + occasional long X threads are enough for foundation phase.

When we do write longer pieces:

**Preferred topics**
- “Why we refuse national averages as the baseline”
- “The exact cost structure of one modular unit (and what can go wrong)”
- “How we will detect ghost units and photo theater”
- Field reports with primary data (once available)
- Open post-mortems of decisions that failed

**Distribution**
- Publish the full text on GitHub (or a simple /writing path later).
- Share the link on X with a short, factual summary.
- Offer the piece to relevant newsletters or forums only after it is already public (no exclusive first rights that delay openness).

## Direct outreach (high value, low volume)

Personal, specific messages to people who can actually help:

- Agronomists and researchers working on smallholder systems in southern Africa
- People running open irrigation, seed, or storage projects
- Local Inhambane / Vilanculos contacts once on the ground
- Technical Twitter/X accounts that have written critically about development projects

Template spirit (never copy-paste spam):
> “We’re running an open modular farm pilot in Vilanculos. Everything (BOM, costs, measurement protocol, integrity rules) is public from day one. I’d value your eyes on [specific document]. No ask beyond critique.”

Never ask for a retweet or “support” in the first message.

---

## Metrics we actually track

We care about:

- Quality of inbound questions and critiques (especially technical)
- Number of people who open and comment on the GitHub documents
- Real local contacts and supplier quotes obtained
- Whether serious people save or bookmark the UNIT / INTEGRITY / PLAN docs

We do **not** optimize for:
- Follower count
- Impressions
- “Engagement rate”
- Viral coefficient

If a post gets high reach but zero thoughtful replies, it was probably the wrong post.

---

## Timeline of effort

| Phase | Promotion focus |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Now (Foundation)** | Document quality + occasional high-signal X posts + targeted outreach to technical people |
| **Ground trip** | Daily field notes, real prices, baseline observations |
| **First units in ground** | Measurement updates, problems encountered, cost actuals |
| **Season 1 data** | Full public results (good or bad). This is when broader amplification becomes legitimate |

Amplification before real data is mostly noise. We accept lower visibility now in exchange for credibility later.
