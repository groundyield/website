# Consent, privacy, and safeguarding v0

**GroundYield public policy**  
Last updated: 27 July 2026

We plan to publish costs, yields, and (where relevant) household income *changes*. That requires clear consent and minimization of harm. This document is the baseline until a formal entity and legal review exist.

---

## Principles

1. **Consent before collection** for personal stories, names, photos of people, GPS of homes, and household income.  
2. **Minimize** — prefer unit IDs and aggregate stats over full names in public CSVs.  
3. **Purpose limitation** — data collected for the pilot and public learning, not resale.  
4. **Right to withdraw** — a participant can ask to stop future collection; already-published open data may need redaction discussion case by case.  
5. **Children** — no identifiable images or personal data of minors in public materials without explicit guardian consent and a clear need.  
6. **Safety over scoop** — land disputes, threats, or sensitive politics stay out of the public git until safe.

---

## What may be public by default

| Data | Public? |
|------|---------|
| Unit design, BOM, supplier price *ranges* | Yes |
| Anonymized yields and costs per unit ID | Yes (with consent to participate in pilot) |
| Failure notes (equipment, agronomy) | Yes — without humiliating individuals |
| Names of partner orgs | Only with org consent |
| Household income exact figures | Only with explicit consent; prefer ranges or % change |
| Chat logs with AI advice | **No** by default ([AI_AGRONOMY.md](AI_AGRONOMY.md)) |

---

## Field process (intent)

1. Explain the pilot in plain language (**Portuguese** when working with local households).  
2. Explain what will be published and what will not.  
3. Record consent (written or documented verbal with witness — method to be fixed on trip).  
4. Assign unit ID; keep linkage key offline if needed.  
5. Re-check consent before publishing photos of people or income detail.

---

## Photos and video

- Prefer crops, equipment, and landscapes.  
- People in frame: consent; no “poverty tourism” framing.  
- Credit and dignity over drama.

---

## Safeguarding

If we become aware of exploitation, coercion, or abuse linked to pilot activities, we stop the relevant activity, protect affected people as far as we can, and do not treat it as content. Contact: team@groundyield.org.

---

## Status

v0 — policy intent before first household enrollment. Will be revised with local partners and, when formed, legal counsel.
