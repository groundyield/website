## Purpose

1. Separate **travel ops** money from **unit capital** (irrigation kits, etc.).  
2. Force a ceiling before the road so scope stays honest.  
3. Leave a public trail of assumptions that can be corrected with receipts.

Currency for this envelope: **USD equivalent** (note ZAR / MZN / USD as used).  
Mark each line **F** (fixed quote) · **E** (estimate) · **H** (hearsay).
