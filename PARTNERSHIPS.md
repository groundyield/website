# Community & Partnership Principles v0

**GroundYield public commitments for how we work with people and land**  
Last updated: 27 July 2026

The pilot only works if land access and relationships are legitimate. This document states **how we intend to partner** before any unit is installed.

---

## Non-negotiables

1. **Local first** — farming with local households and communities, not imported labor models that displace them.  
2. **Consent** — clear agreement before data collection, photos, or publishing stories.  
3. **No land grabs** — we do not seek freehold extraction; access models must be understood and accepted locally.  
4. **Transparency** — material costs and results are public; private performance reports are out of scope (see [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md)).  
5. **Exit with dignity** — if a partnership fails, we document why and leave relationships cleaner than we found them.

---

## Who we partner with

| Partner type | Role |
|--------------|------|
| Smallholder households | Primary operators of modular units |
| Local associations / leaders | Legitimacy, introductions, conflict mediation |
| Suppliers & technicians | Irrigation, inputs, maintenance |
| Extension / NGOs / research | Agronomy depth, language, training |
| Markets (incl. tourism buyers) | Offtake for cash crops — commercial, not charity theater |
| Funders / donors | Capital under open-data terms |

---

## Land access models under consideration

Exact model will be chosen with communities. Options to evaluate on the ground trip:

| Model | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Household land + unit agreement | Farmer owns/uses land; package is support + measurement |
| Association-coordinated plots | Shared rules, individual or group plots |
| Lease / use-right with written terms | Only with clear local process |
| Demo plot + satellite household units | Early visibility without over-centralizing |

**We will not pretend a model is “community-led” if decisions are only made off-site.**

---

## Data & dignity

- Prefer **unit IDs** over full names in public CSVs  
- Sensitive disputes stay out of public git until parties agree  
- Community agreements may be summarized publicly even when full legal text stays private for safety  
- Failure stories are published **without humiliating individuals**

---

## Money & expectations

- Be explicit about what the pilot pays for (kit, inputs, training) vs what the household contributes (land, labor, care)  
- No hidden side deals that contradict public cost tables  
- If subsidies exist, label them so replication math stays honest  

---

## Engagement sequence (Phase 0 → 1)

1. Introductions through trusted local channels  
2. Listen: constraints, history of failed projects, priorities  
3. Share charter and unit design in plain language (Portuguese)  
4. Co-define first 1–few units, not 60 on day one  
5. Baseline measurement with consent  
6. Install → advise → publish  

---

## Red flags (walk away or redesign)

- Pressure to skip baseline measurement  
- Requests to hide costs or failures  
- Land conflict we cannot understand  
- Exclusive lock-in that blocks farmer exit or replication  
- Advice that only works with permanent external subsidy with no path to honesty about it
