# Modular unit partnership — draft outline v0

**Status:** Public discussion draft — **not a signed contract**, not legal advice.  
**Purpose:** State intended terms in plain language before any unit is installed.  
Final agreements will be written with local partners and, when formed, legal counsel.

Related: [PARTNERSHIPS.md](PARTNERSHIPS.md) · [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) · [CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md) · [UNIT.md](UNIT.md)

---

## Parties (concept)

| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| GroundYield (operator: Jacques Theron) | Package design, measurement, public reporting, agreed capital support |
| Household / group operator | Land access (as agreed), daily farming, care of equipment, data participation |
| Local association / leaders (if any) | Legitimacy, introductions, conflict mediation |

---

## Intent of the partnership

1. Deploy a **measured modular package** (irrigation + inputs + advice + storage as agreed).  
2. Record **baseline** before claiming improvement.  
3. Publish **costs, yields, and failures** using unit IDs — not humiliating personal detail.  
4. Leave the household **better able to continue**, not dependent on secrecy.  
5. Follow [INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md): no fake baselines, no ghost units, honest reporting both ways.

---

## What GroundYield may provide (case by case)

- Technical design of the modular unit  
- Agreed share of capital items and Season 1 inputs (listed in writing)  
- Agronomy reminders (WhatsApp/SMS) after consent  
- Help logging yields and costs for the public dataset  

Exact money and items are **never verbal-only** — write a short annex per unit.

---

## What the household / group provides

- Legitimate access to the plot under local rules  
- Labour and day-to-day decisions on the crop mix within the measured package  
- Honest reporting of yields and major problems  
- Care of shared or loaned equipment as agreed  

---

## Data & consent

- Participation in public data uses a **unit ID** by default  
- Names, faces, income detail: only with clear consent ([CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md))  
- Right to stop future collection; discuss redaction of sensitive published items case by case  

---

## Money clarity

| Topic | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Kit ownership | State whether items are gift, loan, co-owned, or phased transfer |
| Side deals | None that contradict public cost tables |
| Failure | Equipment failure is documented; blame is not the product |
| Exit | Either party can end; equipment and data handling written in the annex |

---

## Dispute path (intent)

1. Direct conversation.  
2. Local association / trusted mediator if both agree.  
3. Pause public storytelling that could inflame the dispute.  
4. Document outcome in public summary when safe.
