## 0. How to read this

### Purpose

1. List **what can kill or corrupt** this pilot (including self-deception).  
2. Show **where we are now** vs what “ready for unit capital” requires (**gaps**).  
3. Bind each major risk to a **mitigation**, an **owner**, a **gate**, and a **stop rule**.  

### Scoring (simple, public)

| Score | Likelihood (L) | Impact (I) |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| **1** | Unlikely this season | Inconvenience; redo paper |
| **2** | Possible | Delay / money loss under ~1 unit cost |
| **3** | Likely without control | Unit fails; household harmed; trust hit |
| **4** | Almost certain if we ignore history | Pilot collapse; integrity breach; repeat of prior loss pattern |

**Priority = L × I** (max 16). Treat **≥9** as **must-control before unit capital leaves**.  
Residual risk after controls is **R** (same 1–4 × 1–4 product, honest).

### Roles

| Role | Who (now) |
|------|-----------|
| **Owner** | Jacques Theron unless named otherwise |
| **Field co-owner** | Named household/partner **after** G1/G4 (not yet) |
| **Public challenger** | Anyone via GitHub issue / email ([INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md)) |

### What this is not

- Not insurance, legal advice, or a guarantee of safety or yield  
- Not a claim that residual risk is zero  
- Not a substitute for local law, DUAT, or community process

## 6. Residual risks we accept (honest)

We **cannot** design away:

1. **Climate shocks** (cyclone, drought, flood)  
2. **Some theft** despite controls (crime is real)  
3. **Remote operator bandwidth** (HSE job in KSA)  
4. **Policy / cost environment** that already hurt at scale once  
5. **Market surprise** after good faith buyer notes  
6. **Self-report era** until external audit exists  

Acceptance rule: **size small, publish fast, stop clean** — do not “accept” by pretending impact.
