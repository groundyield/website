# Outreach — how we ask for attention without hype

**Public playbook**  
Last updated: 27 July 2026

Companion to [TRACTION.md](TRACTION.md).  
**One rule:** lead with **process and honesty**, not the charter’s unproven numbers.

We do **not** promote “80–100% yield” or “30–60 units” as if measured. Those are **design aims** ([CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md)). Traction that pretends otherwise is a trust failure waiting to happen.

---

## Why this angle

This project is **one day old** as a public identity (domain + GitHub + docs same window).  
There is **no** local partner on WHO, **no** baseline, **no** supplier quote, **no** trip date, **no** external auditor.

Amplifying vision-numbers on that base looks like every other development theater launch.  
Amplifying **“built in public, here’s what’s unproven, pressure-test us”** is on-brand and cannot be “walked back” later for overclaiming.

## What we promote (priority order)

1. **Honest stage** — foundation phase; zero units; zero baselines; trip dates open  
2. **Integrity / anti-fraud** — [INTEGRITY.md](INTEGRITY.md), public [units.html](https://www.groundyield.org/units.html) count = 0  
3. **Open design** — [UNIT.md](UNIT.md) BOM, cost **planning** bands (labeled as such)  
4. **Field kit ready** — offline forms; no farmers messaged with agronomy yet (freeze)  
5. **Named operator** — Jacques Theron, self-funded, no legal entity yet ([WHO.md](WHO.md))  
6. **Specific asks** (below) — not “support us” or money  

## What we do not promote (yet)

- Yield % as a result  
- Unit counts > 0  
- “Proven model” / “ready to scale”  
- Urgency, scarcity, FOMO  
- Agronomy WhatsApp as if live service
