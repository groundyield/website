## Principles

1. **People over content** — no photo, quote, or scoop is worth coercion or danger.  
2. **Local knowledge first** — ask hosts about roads, water, politics, health, **theft patterns**.  
3. **Redundancy** — offline copies of forms, IDs, contacts, and this checklist.  
4. **Public honesty, private detail** — sensitive incidents can be summarized without endangering anyone.  
5. **Kit security is not trip security** — personal safety (this file) is separate from **asset theft controls** ([SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md)).

## Before departure

- [ ] Valid travel documents for SA / ZW / MZ as required; copies offline + cloud  
- [ ] Vaccinations / malaria advice checked for season (personal medical decision)  
- [ ] Travel insurance decision recorded (yes/no + policy ref offline)  
- [ ] Emergency contacts (family + one trip buddy) know rough route and check-in cadence  
- [ ] `team@groundyield.org` reachable or auto-reply set if offline for days  
- [ ] Cash plan + card backup; never all liquidity in one place  
- [ ] Offline maps / downloaded region; paper sketch of Vilanculos area if needed  
- [ ] Field PDFs + forms printed + on phone ([field/FIELD_KIT.md](field/FIELD_KIT.md))  
- [ ] Phone lock + encrypted notes for private names  
- [ ] Basic first-aid kit and water strategy  

---

## Daily while in field

- [ ] Morning: location plan + who you meet (private notes OK)  
- [ ] Check-in with buddy on agreed schedule  
- [ ] Night: backup photos of paper forms; battery charge  
- [ ] No night travel on unfamiliar roads when avoidable  
- [ ] Decline meetings that feel extractive, rushed, or unsafe

## Communications ladder

| Level | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Normal | Daily or every-other-day check-in with buddy |
| Delayed (no signal) | Pre-agreed “silent up to N hours” window |
| Incident | Contact local hosts → buddy → emergency services as needed |
| Public note | Short factual line in GROUND_TRIP when safe (no operational security leaks) |

## Data & device safety

- Private names and phone numbers stay **off** public git  
- Consent before photos of people ([field/CONSENT_SCRIPT.md](field/CONSENT_SCRIPT.md))  
- If a device is lost: remote lock if available; assume private notes compromised  
- Prefer unit IDs in anything that might be screenshared  

---

## Stop rules (pause the pilot activity)

Stop or leave if you see:

- Coercion around land or water access  
- Threats, weapons, or escalating conflict  
- Requests to hide costs or fake yields  
- Exploitation of minors or clear safeguarding issues → [CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md)

Publish later: what was learned **without** endangering people.

## After incident

1. Safety first.  
2. Private log (time, place, facts).  
3. Public summary only when safe and useful.  
4. Correct any published claim that no longer holds.  
5. If **kit theft/vandalism**: follow [SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md) § Incident response (serials, leaders, no public suspect names).
