## 1. Growth model — no real fallback past unit one

**Problem:** Funding unit two leans on (a) household self-fund season 2 (needs cash crop/tourism markets that are **unverified**), (b) named grant (not currently soliciting), or (c) operator salary after a large prior loss. If veg/tourism fails, “open replication by others” is not a capital plan.

**Decision recorded (design):**

| Path | Role |
|------|------|
| **Plan A** | Food-security unit first; cash is bonus if offtake works |
| **Plan B** | **Stop at 1–few learning units** funded as R&D; publish autopsy; no forced scale |
| **Plan C** | Named external capital only under integrity terms (WHO) — not fake yields |
| **Not a plan** | Promise “each unit pays for three more” without F prices |

Detail: [VALUE_AND_MONEY.md](VALUE_AND_MONEY.md) §4.5.

## 2. Trip scope — two weeks vs re-earning trust

**Problem:** Docs say trust must be **re-earned**, but the same window also lists land, water, quotes, baseline, ownership model, annex, maybe install.

**Decision recorded:**

> **This ground window is primarily a listening + reconnaissance trip.**  
> **G1** (real intro) is the must-hit. **G2** (quotes) and baseline *starts* are stretch.  
> **No unit install** unless water F + legitimacy + annex + capital checklist are *already* true — unlikely in first re-entry after scars.  
> Install is a **second-visit / later window** default.

See [GROUND_TRIP.md](GROUND_TRIP.md) · [NEXT.md](NEXT.md).

## 4. “Near impossible” vs charter 80–100% / 30–60 units

**Problem:** LESSONS says large **commercial SA-style** farming felt near-impossible under conditions hit. CHARTER still aims large modular scale and big yield %. Careful readers feel a clash.

**Reconciliation:**

| Claim | Scope |
|-------|--------|
| Near-impossible | **Empire-scale commercial** (large ha, opaque, high fixed cost) — lived |
| Still worth testing | **Tiny modular measured packages** — different risk shape, **not proven** |
| 80–100% / 30–60 | **Aspirational hypotheses** if gates and capital allow — **not** a promise of empire success |

Charter language tightened: [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md).
