## 4. “Near-impossible” vs modular — reframe is **not** resolution

| What we claimed | What is true |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Empire-scale commercial was near-impossible under conditions hit | Lived, E |
| Tiny modular is a *different risk shape* | **Hypothesis only** — not demonstrated |
| Small unit avoids 100% loss | **False as absolute:** theft of a ~$1–2k kit is still **100% loss of that capital** (T1/T2 P=12). Water unknown (A1 P=12) still kills the package |

**Honest position:** modular may **bound absolute $ at risk per bet** and improve learning speed; it does **not** remove the causal mechanisms (theft, water, markets, cost, institutions) that made large ops hard. Those risks **scale down proportionally** and can still zero a household package. Success is **not assumed** from smaller size.

Charter aims remain aspirational hypotheses under that honesty.
