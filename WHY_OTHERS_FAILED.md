# Why so many tried — and failed — in Mozambique agri

**Public synthesis for GroundYield design**  
Last updated: 27 July 2026  
**Truth:** Secondary sources (H) + operator lived experience (E until records).  
**Not:** a claim that GroundYield is immune. **Is:** a map of known failure modes so we design against them.

## 1. The pattern (many tried)

Mozambique repeatedly attracts:

| Wave | Example pattern | Common outcome |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| SA commercial settlers | 1990s programmes (e.g. Mosagrius / SACADA-type schemes) | Many left within years |
| Cross-border commercial farmers | Zimbabwean / SA operators into Manica and other provinces | Boom–bust cycles |
| Large land / biofuel / export projects | Concession-style investments | Many abandoned or contested |
| NGO / government input projects | Pumps, seed, “packages” | High failure when training & ownership thin |
| Operator’s own prior bet | Large ha + livestock + delivery (Vilanculos area) | Exit ~5 years ago; heavy personal loss |

**Smallholders** still produce most food; **commercial** pockets remain small relative to potential. Productivity stays low; risk is high (credit, markets, climate, institutions).

## 2. Failure modes (what the data and history keep showing)

### A. Infrastructure and logistics

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Roads / cold chain weak | Harvest spoils; inputs late | Small units near markets; don’t depend on “promised roads” |
| Power unreliable | Diesel / cold store fails | Solar core; size for reality |
| Distance to Maputo/Beira markets | Margin eaten by transport | Test **local + tourism** offtake first |

Historical note: commercial settlers often counted on **government infrastructure promises** that did not arrive; at least one reported full crop loss when chemicals arrived too late.

### B. Finance and cost ramps

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Credit never arrives | Farms undercapitalised mid-build | Self-fund pilot scale; no fake loan promises |
| Costs spike (fuel, import, fees, policy) | Cash-flow death | Public quotes; stop rules; small modular not empire |
| Single-crop export bets | Price or logistics shock kills farm | Diversify: staple + veg + optional livestock |
| High interest / weak enforcement | No bankable story | Open data for funders; household surplus first |

Operator lived **cost/government pressure post-COVID** plus bad choices — see [LESSONS_MZ.md](LESSONS_MZ.md).

### C. Land and community conflict

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Unclear DUAT / community rights | Conflict, blockades, exit | Household land + written legitimacy; no grab |
| Unpaid labour / “arrogant” outsider style | Social breakdown | Local first; Portuguese; Rumbacaca relationship |
| Land for investors vs locals | Political blowback | 0.25–0.5 ha modular on community terms |

### D. Skills and “start from zero”

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Assume SA conditions | Wrong crop/timing/disease | Local baseline first |
| Don’t speak Portuguese | Isolation | PT materials; local partners |
| Bush clearing / setup underestimated | Startup capital burns before planting | Don’t promise instant scale |

### E. Technology without ownership (esp. solar irrigation)

From solar irrigation market work in Mozambique and the region:

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Theft / vandalism of panels and pumps** | Kit disappears; project dies | See **[SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md)** |
| Many pumps, little training | Failure “within a very short time” | Train custodians; few units first |
| Portable vs fixed trade-off | Fixed = stealable; portable = less convenient | Prefer **removable / storable** where theft high |
| No spare parts / service | Dead kit in field | Quote local service; public dead-ends |

Energypedia / Practica-type summaries on solar irrigation in Mozambique flag **theft risk as a major constraint** for solar pumps.

### F. Markets and offtake

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| No buyer for surplus | “Success” harvest, zero cash | Buyer interviews before scale |
| Tourism demand assumed | Seasonal / quality mismatch | Measure real lodge/restaurant demand |
| Middlemen capture margin | Farmer still poor | Log farm-gate prices honestly |

### G. Governance and mega-programmes

| Failure | What happens | GroundYield response |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Large state programmes under-deliver | Weak results despite big banners | Small, measured, public autopsies |
| Abandoned concessions | Land “invested” then empty | We claim only **registry** units |
| Subsidy without exit path | Collapse when donor leaves | Label subsidies; household season-2 path |

## 3. Why “many tried” is not an argument to quit — or to copy them

| Copying failed models | Learning from them |
|----------------------|--------------------|
| Big land first | Small modular first |
| Opaque private pain | Public costs and failures |
| Outsider-only control | Community operates the plot |
| Fixed solar “gift” farms | Ownership + theft design |
| Promise then leave | Stop rules before enroll |
| Empire recovery fantasy | Open-source package others can run |

## 4. Crime and theft

**Dedicated controls:** [SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md)

One-line principle:

> If the kit is easier to steal than to earn from, it will be stolen. Design so the **community loses more by theft than by honest use** — and so the kit can be **moved, shared, or recovered** without a war.

## 6. Design checklist (from failures)

Before any unit capital leaves:

- [ ] Local legitimacy (not fence-first)  
- [ ] Water source real (not assumed)  
- [ ] Theft plan written ([SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md))  
- [ ] Training named person for kit  
- [ ] Offtake hypothesis tested (even one buyer interview)  
- [ ] Cost quote F/E with date  
- [ ] Exit if operator remote for months  
- [ ] Public registry only after install  

---

**Bottom line:** Others failed from **logistics, cost ramps, land conflict, thin markets, no training, and theft** — not because modular open farming was tried and disproven. We only win if we **design against those modes**, especially crime.
