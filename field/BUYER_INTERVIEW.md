## Questions

| # | Prompt | Capture |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | What fresh produce do you buy most weeks? | crops list |
| 2 | Volume per week / month (local units OK) | qty + unit · F/E/H |
| 3 | Preferred quality / size / packaging? | specs |
| 4 | Typical price range + currency + seasonality? | price table |
| 5 | Who do you buy from today (type: farm / market / trader)? | channel |
| 6 | Reliability problems (stockouts, quality, transport)? | pain |
| 7 | Interest in a steady local grower with irrigation? | interest · not commitment |
| 8 | Payment terms usual (cash, weekly, invoice)? | terms |
| 9 | Can we cite your **business type** publicly (e.g. “hotel, Vilanculos”)? | consent |
| 10 | Best follow-up channel? | private notes only |

- Quote UNIT planning costs as customer prices
