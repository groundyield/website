## Handout principles (do not dilute)

- Season 1 yield target vs **local baseline**, not national averages  
- Cost band is **planning**, not a raised budget or payment offer  
- Jacques Theron named; self-funded; **no legal entity yet**  
- **What we are not asking today** — no money, no final agreement on first contact
