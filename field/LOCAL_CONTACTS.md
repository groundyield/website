## How to use (2-week trip)

| Priority | Day type | Who |
|----------|----------|-----|
| **P1** | First 2 days in Vilanculos | Hardware / solar-ish retail · municipal / extension · market agro stalls · 2–3 lodges (produce offtake) |
| **P2** | Mid window | Inhambane town if logistics allow · more lodges/restaurants · water/borehole drillers |
| **P3** | Maputo or SA transit | National seed/fertilizer/solar (quotes for planning) |
| **P4** | Remote before/after | Email Futurepump / Lorentz / PICS / GrainPro (already in QUOTE_REQUESTS) |

**Ask order for every business:** (1) do you sell X, (2) written price, (3) delivery to Vilanculos area, (4) lead time, (5) can we cite you publicly as “dealer type, town”.

## A. Vilanculos / Vilankulo town (walk first)

### A1. Hardware · building · electrical · solar-adjacent

| Name / type | Why contact | Public find | Ask for | Status |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|---------|--------|
| **Build it / Construa Build it Vilanculos** | Building materials chain; pipe, fittings, basic hardware, sometimes pumps | Search: *Build it Vilanculos* / buildit.co.za international stores | Pipe, fittings, tanks, any pump/solar stock; **written price list** | lead |
| **Vilankulo Electro World** | Local hardware / electrical / cross-border supplies | Instagram `@vew_vilankulo` · search Facebook/maps | Solar panels, cable, pumps, waterproofing | lead |
| **Other hardware on main roads / market edge** | Same category — names change; walk the strip | In-person | Same | open |
| **Local borehole / well drillers** | Water access reality for irrigation | Ask hardware + lodges for “quem fura furos” | Depth typical, cost band, success rate (E/H) | open |

### A2. Agro-inputs (seed · fertilizer · chemicals)

| Name / type | Why contact | Public find | Ask for | Status |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|---------|--------|
| **Agro-stalls at Vilanculos market** | Actual smallholder channel | Walk morning market | Maize/veg seed brands, fertilizer bag prices, seasonality | open |
| **Any shop stocking Yara / Bayer / Syngenta / Klein Karoo / EASI / local blend brands** | Brands named in MZ agro-dealer reports | Ask “onde comprar adubo e semente” | Stock + price + origin | open |
| **Veterinary / livestock kiosk** (if any) | Poultry module later | Town walk | Day-old chicks, feed | open |

*Note: National brand names (Yara Mozambique, Agrifocus/UPL, SEMOC, Mozfert) are often **distributors** in Maputo/Beira/Chimoio — use them for planning quotes if no local stock.*
