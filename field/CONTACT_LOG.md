# Contact log (public-safe)

Use this to track **G1** progress without putting private phone numbers in git.

**Private detail** (names, phones): offline notes only.  
**Public summary:** role/org type + date + outcome → paste a one-liner into [GROUND_TRIP.md](../GROUND_TRIP.md) when material.

---

## Rules

1. No private numbers in this file.  
2. Org names only with consent or if already public.  
3. “Interested” ≠ partner — partner needs WHO.md consent.  
4. Dead ends stay (do not delete).  
5. Agronomy send still frozen ([ADVICE_SCRIPTS.md](../ADVICE_SCRIPTS.md)).

## Outcomes glossary

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| no reply | Wait or one polite follow-up after 7+ days |
| declined | Closed — note why if known |
| call booked | Meeting date (public only if useful) |
| quote promised | Chase into SUPPLIERS / quotes CSV |
| intro offered | Track who they introduce (still private until consent) |
| closed | Dead end |

---

## When to claim “local engagement started”

Only after ≥1 row with a real outcome beyond “no reply”, and a dated public note in GROUND_TRIP (gate **G1**).
