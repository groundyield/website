## 0. The hard truth first

Communities have seen projects come and go: free seeds, broken pumps, NGOs that leave, commercial farms that fence land and fail.

**Jacques already left once** after a large commercial bet collapsed (~ZAR 10m operator-disclosed loss). That history cuts both ways:

| Why they might still listen | Why they might say no |
|-----------------------------|------------------------|
| They **know him** (esp. Rumbacaca) | He left when it got hard |
| He is not a stranger with a clipboard | Trust must be **re-earned** |
| New model is **small, open, measured** | “Why believe this time?” |
| He says **no free-money pitch on day one** | Some want gifts, not partnership |

GroundYield only works if the answer to “what’s in it for us?” is **clearer than the risk of disappointment**.

## 1. From the community’s POV — why let us help?

People do not “let us help” out of charity. They engage if **they gain more than they risk**.

### What they risk

| Risk | Honest mitigation |
|------|-------------------|
| Wasted time / meetings | Short meetings; no pressure; they can walk away |
| Land or water captured by outsiders | Household keeps land control; no freehold grab ([PARTNERSHIPS.md](PARTNERSHIPS.md)) |
| Broken kit left on them | Ownership written **before** install; repair plan; public failure log |
| Debt they can’t pay | Prefer grant/pilot capital or phased co-pay — **not** predatory loans |
| Data / photos used against them | Consent; unit IDs; they can refuse ([CONSENT.md](CONSENT.md)) |
| Another abandonment | Stop rules public; no enroll if operator can’t support ([WHO.md](WHO.md)) |

### What can be in it for them (the “init”)

| Benefit | How it shows up | When real |
|---------|-----------------|-----------|
| **More food** | Higher staple yield vs **their** baseline | After unit + season (measured) |
| **More cash** | Irrigated veg / poultry sold locally or to tourism | Only if market works (verify) |
| **Water** | Solar pump when rain fails | After install + working well/source |
| **Skills** | Training, package discipline, storage | During season |
| **Less loss** | Hermetic bags | First harvest after storage |
| **Voice / dignity** | Results published without humiliation | Always if they join |
| **School connectivity** (optional) | Starlink hub for learning — separate track | Only if path funded/written |
| **Option to exit** | Kit rules pre-agreed; not trapped | Written annex |

**We do not buy loyalty with mystery money.** We co-invest in a **package they operate**, and we publish whether it worked.

### Why work with *this* person (Jacques)

| Reason | Limit |
|--------|--------|
| Known face in Rumbacaca | Not automatic yes forever |
| Lived the cost of failure here | Not proof the new model works |
| Open books ambition | Must keep doing it |
| HSE / industrial discipline abroad | Time in-country is limited — say so |
| Small modular start | Not a return of 40k ha dreams |

**First ask is not “take our kit.”** First ask is: *listen, measure what you already grow, decide if a 0.25–0.5 ha package is worth trying.*

## 2. What protects the community

| Protection | Mechanism |
|------------|-----------|
| Consent | Photos, names, income — only with yes |
| Land stays theirs | Pilot does not seek to seize land |
| Written annex | What we pay for vs what they provide |
| Public costs | Side deals that contradict tables are banned |
| Baseline honesty | No fake “miracle” vs invented low baseline |
| Exit | Either party can end under written rules |
| Dignity | Failures published without shaming families |
| No mystery agents | Official contact team@groundyield.org only |

### 2.1 Household selection (fairness)

Personal history in Rumbacaca is a **door**, not a **crown**. Selection that only rewards “people Jacques already likes” recreates elite capture (risk C3).

| Principle | Practice (v0) |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Transparent criteria** | Water access, household willingness, land use OK, ability to care for kit — written in plain language |
| **Local witness** | Named association / elders / church or school committee — **who** is written in annex for that village |
| **Not only old friends** | At least discuss one household **outside** the closest personal circle for unit 2+ |
| **Dispute** | Written: complain to local witness + team@groundyield.org; pause install if unresolved |
| **Publish process** | Summary of *how* chosen (not private gossip) after consent |

Detail: [PARTNERSHIPS.md](PARTNERSHIPS.md). If no legitimate local forum exists, **do not multi-household roll out** — stay at demo/listen.

## 3. What protects Jacques and the money spent

Capital is scarce (self-funded + day job). Protection is **process**, not legal fortress (no entity yet).

### 3.1 Before any money leaves the account

| Gate | Why |
|------|-----|
| Real intro + consent | No cold install |
| Baseline started | Know starting point |
| Written unit annex | Kit list, ownership, care, exit |
| Site water reality | Don’t buy wrong pump |
| Quote on kit items | Don’t overpay blind |
| Fit with time in-country | Don’t promise weekly visits you can’t keep |

### 3.1b Theft and capital destruction

A stolen panel or pump is **total capital loss** for that unit — and a community story that “projects bring thieves.”  
Controls are first-class design, not afterthought: **[SECURITY_THEFT.md](SECURITY_THEFT.md)**.  
Principle: stake before steel · earn > steal · moveable kit · few units · public serials · no automatic free replace.

### 3.2 Ownership models (pick per unit — write it down)

| Model | Community gets | Capital protection | Best when |
|-------|----------------|--------------------|-----------|
| **A. Pilot grant kit** | Use of kit; may keep after season if rules met | Loss is pilot R&D; public data is the return | First 1–few units; learning |
| **B. Co-invest** | Share of capital; rest from household/group | Shared skin in game | Household has some cash/labour surplus |
| **C. Loan of kit** | Use for season(s); return or buy-out | Asset can move to next unit if care fails | Clear inventory control |
| **D. Phased transfer** | Earn ownership via care + data + seasons | Encourages stewardship | Longer relationship |

**Default for early Rumbacaca units (proposal to discuss, not fixed):**  
**A or D** — small number, public learning, transfer if they keep package alive.  
**Not** large debt books. **Not** “free forever, no rules.”

### 3.3 What we spend money *on* (and not on)

| Spend | Don’t spend |
|-------|-------------|
| Agreed kit items on annex | Cash “allowances” without deliverable |
| Training / shared tools | Land purchase for extraction |
| Measurement (bags, scales, notebooks) | Hidden side payments |
| Repair that teaches a lesson | Endless replace without root cause |

### 3.4 What “return” we accept on early capital

Early capital may **not** repay in cash. Acceptable returns for pilot phase:

1. **Public data** others can copy (open-source food)  
2. **Proof** the package works or fails in Rumbacaca conditions  
3. **Relationships** that enable honest expansion  
4. **Kit still working** under community care  

If the only acceptable return is “get rich in Moz farming,” history already answered that ([LESSONS_MZ.md](LESSONS_MZ.md)).

### 3.5 Operator protections (practical)

| Protection | Note |
|------------|------|
| Public stop rules | Pause new units if funding/time dies |
| No unlimited liability promises | Annex lists exact items |
| Remote job disclosed | Communities know he is not always present |
| Integrity / no impostors | Only official channels |
| Mediation path | Local leaders before escalation |
| Legal entity later | When scale needs contracts/insurance |

**No entity yet** = weaker legal shield. Compensate with **small scale**, **written annexes**, **public process**.

### 3.6 Personal financial ceiling (required before unit capital)

“No continuous dependency” is empty without a number Jacques will not exceed.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Before unit money leaves** | Publish on [WHO.md](WHO.md): max pilot kit capital for **2026** (exact or band) **or** state “ceiling not set — **no unit install**” |
| **What it covers** | Modular unit kit + season-1 inputs on annex — not unlimited repairs forever |
| **What it does not cover** | Unlimited Starlink for every school; empire rebuild |
| **If hit** | Stop new units; support only what annex already promises; public note |

**Current:** ceiling **not yet published** — therefore **no unit capital** until set. (Open bracket is intentional honesty, not permission to spend unbounded.)

## 4. The economy — how money keeps helping *more* communities

Charity that only spends never scales. Extraction that only extracts destroys trust.  
GroundYield needs a **loop** where value created can fund the next unit.

### 4.1 Three loops (can coexist)

```
LOOP 1 — HOUSEHOLD SURPLUS (primary long-term)
  Kit + water + inputs → more food/cash for household
  → household can self-fund season 2 inputs
  → pilot capital free for NEXT household

LOOP 2 — OPEN-SOURCE REPLICATION (mission)
  Public BOM + real costs + failures
  → other funders/NGOs/farmers copy without us
  → impact without our balance sheet

LOOP 3 — STRUCTURED RECOVERY (optional later)
  Kit co-pay / phased buy-out / small revolving fund
  → recovered $ → next unit
  → only with clear, fair terms (no debt traps)
```

### 4.2 Illustrative unit math (not a promise)

From [ECONOMICS.md](ECONOMICS.md) mid planning bands:

| | USD (order of magnitude) |
|--|--------------------------|
| Year-0 kit (mid) | ~2,000 |
| Illustrative surplus if veg markets work | ~few hundred / year |
| Staple-only payback | Often **slow** |

**Design implication:**  

- Food security gains may matter more than fast cash payback.  
- Cash to fund “next community” needs **cash crops + markets** *or* **outside capital** (named grants) *or* **replication by others**.  
- Do not promise “each unit pays for three more” until field numbers exist.

### 4.5 Plan B if cash crops / tourism offtake fail

The path past unit one **must not** silently assume lodge menus and perfect veg markets.

| If… | Then… |
|-----|--------|
| Offtake prices thin or zero | Unit 1 is still valid as **food + learning + public data** (Plan A food-security) |
| Household cannot self-fund season 2 inputs | **Do not shame**; pilot may fund a second input season **only** if ceiling allows and annex says so — or stop cleanly |
| No grant appears | **Default: stop scaling.** Publish season autopsy. Open BOM remains the “next community” offer |
| Operator cannot fund unit 2 | Say so on WHO; **no ghost pipeline** of “coming soon” units |
| Markets work (F prices) | Revisit Loop 1 surplus and optional recovery (Loop 3) with real numbers |

**Honest end-state if Plan B is forever:** a handful of measured units and an open playbook — **not** 30–60 units. That is success for integrity even if it fails the charter *aim*.

See also [STRATEGIC_GAPS.md](STRATEGIC_GAPS.md) §1.

### 4.3 Funding the next community (priority order)

| Source | When ethical | Risk |
|--------|--------------|------|
| **1. Household self-funds season 2+** | Best | None for us; may be hard for them |
| **2. Named grant / donor** (WHO disclosure) | Pilot scale-up | Donor pressure for pretty results — resist |
| **3. Phased kit recovery** (co-pay / buy-out) | After trust + surplus | Looks like debt if poorly designed |
| **4. Operator self-fund more units** | Slow, honest | Limited by KSA job + past losses |
| **5. “Franchise” / open copy** | Others implement | Quality control |

**Starlink / school kits** should be **separate budgets** (education path), not stolen from farm unit capital — unless a donor funds both under open rules.

### 4.4 What we will not do to “make money”

| Forbidden | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Sell community land stories for vanity fundraising | Integrity |
| Fake yield % to unlock grants | Integrity |
| Lock farmers into exclusive offtake at bad prices | Extraction |
| Charge join-up fees via agents | Fraud pattern |
| Hide subsidies so replication math lies | Breaks open-source promise |

## 5. Simple deal outline (plain language for Rumbacaca)

**For the household / group**

> We can try **one small irrigated package** on land you already control.  
> We measure what you grow **now** first.  
> We agree in writing what equipment is yours to use, who pays what, and what happens if it breaks or if I cannot return for a long time.  
> Results (without shaming you) go public so others learn.  
> You can say no. You can stop.

**For GroundYield**

> We spend only what the annex lists.  
> We learn in public.  
> We do not promise 40,000 ha or guaranteed riches.  
> If it works, the next family can get a package — funded by surplus, recovery, or named capital — not by silence and debt.

## 8. Bottom line

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Why would they work with me? | Known person + small clear package + dignity + optional real water/food/cash gains — if we re-earn trust |
| What’s in it for them? | Package value + skills + exit rights + public respect — not mystery cash |
| What protects money? | Gates, written annex, ownership model, small scale, stop rules, no fake scale |
| How keep helping others? | Household self-fund seasons → open replication → fair recovery → named capital — **measured**, not hoped |

**Open-source food** is the product that survives even if one unit fails: the next community (or country) can read the BOM and the autopsy.
